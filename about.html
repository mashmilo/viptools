About VipTools

VipTools is a free collection of everyday online tools, built to be fast, simple and private. From a word counter and password generator to a JSON formatter, EMI calculator and image compressor, every tool is designed to do one job well without sign-up or clutter.

Our mission

Many useful tasks, like checking a word count, calculating a loan EMI or formatting an API response, should take seconds. We build tools that load quickly, work on phones, tablets and desktops, and give accurate results right away.

Privacy first

Most of our tools run entirely in your browser. The text, numbers and files you enter are processed on your own device and are not uploaded to our servers. You can read more in our Privacy Policy.

What we offer

Our library covers text tools, calculators, developer utilities, converters and generators. New tools are added regularly based on what people need. If you have a suggestion, visit our contact page.

Accuracy and disclaimers

Calculators such as EMI, BMI, compound interest and age calculators provide estimates for general information. They are not financial, medical or legal advice. Please consult a qualified professional before making important decisions.
