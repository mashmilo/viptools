Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

VipTools.online ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how it is used and the choices you have.

Text and files you process

Our tools run in your browser. The text, numbers and images you enter are processed on your device and are not stored or sent to our servers by the tool itself. Some tools save your last inputs in your browser's local storage so they are there when you return. That data stays on your device, and you can clear it through your browser settings.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site, such as which pages are popular and how people arrive. Analytics uses cookies and collects usage data such as pages viewed, approximate location and device type. Where consent is required, analytics cookies are set only after you accept them in our cookie banner.

Search Console

We use Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools to monitor how our pages appear in search results. These services do not collect personal information from visitors.

Advertising

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google and its partners, including DoubleClick, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Where personalised ads are not permitted or you have not consented, ads may be shown without personalisation.

You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting google.com/settings/ads, or by visiting aboutads.info/choices. For more on how Google uses data, see Google's ad technologies policy.

Cookies

Cookies are small files stored on your device. We use cookies for analytics and advertising, which you can accept or reject in our cookie banner. We also store your theme preference (light or dark) and your cookie choice in your browser's local storage. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some features may not work as expected.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data we hold about you, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. Residents of the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and California may have additional rights under the GDPR, UK GDPR or CCPA/CPRA. To exercise any right, email contact@viptools.online. We do not sell personal information.

Third-party links

Our site may link to external websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

Children

Our site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page, and the date at the top shows when it was last updated.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email contact@viptools.online.
