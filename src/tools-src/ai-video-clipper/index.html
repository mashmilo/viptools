Download clip

    The AI Video Clipper helps you cut a short clip from a longer video. Its highlight finder looks at how loud the sound is and how much the picture changes, then suggests the most energetic moments. You can pick a suggestion or set your own start and end times, choose a normal or vertical 9:16 shape, and download the clip. Everything happens in your browser, so your video is never uploaded.

    How the highlight finder works

    The tool reads the audio of your video and measures the loudness every second, then samples frames across the video to see where the picture changes sharply, such as a new scene or a fast cut. It combines both measures into a score and suggests the highest-scoring clips without overlapping. The finder does not understand speech or meaning, so always check the suggestions and adjust the times to suit your content.

    Making a vertical clip

    Choose Vertical 9:16 to crop the centre of your video to the tall shape used by YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and TikTok. The crop keeps the middle of the frame, so move the subject toward the centre before you record if it sits at the edge.

    Recording time and browsers

    A clip is recorded in real time, so a 30 second clip takes about 30 seconds to export. Keep the tab open and in front while it records. The latest versions of Chrome, Edge and Firefox support recording, and Safari may save an MP4 file instead of WebM.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is my video uploaded?

    No. The clipping and highlight analysis run in your browser, and the video stays on your device.

    Why does the highlight finder not pick the right moment?

    It measures sound and picture changes, not what is said or shown. A quiet, important moment can score low, so use the start and end boxes to choose it yourself.

    Which video files work?

    MP4 and WebM files work in most browsers. Some MOV and HEVC files may not open, depending on your browser and device.

    Is there a size limit?

    There is no fixed limit, but the highlight finder works best on videos under 300 MB. Very long videos take longer to analyse on older phones.
