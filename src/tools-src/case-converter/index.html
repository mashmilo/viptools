Case Converter

Convert text to UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case or Sentence case.

The Case Converter changes the capitalisation of any text in one click. It supports uppercase, lowercase, title case and sentence case, and runs privately in your browser.

How to use Case Converter

Paste your text into the box and press the button for the style you want. The text is replaced instantly, and you can switch styles as many times as you like.

Why use Case Converter

Fixing text typed with caps lock on, formatting headlines and tidying copied content by hand is slow. This tool saves time for writers, students, marketers and developers, and consistent capitalisation makes titles and headings look professional.

Frequently asked questions

Is my text stored? No, it never leaves your device. Does title case skip small words? It capitalises every word for simplicity. Is it free? Yes, always.
