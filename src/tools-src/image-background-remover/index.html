Brush:

Remove the background from a photo or product picture in your browser. The tool finds the colour behind the subject, cuts it out, and lets you touch up edges with a brush. Your image is never uploaded.

How to use Image Background Remover

Choose an image and wait a moment while the background colour is detected. If too much or too little disappears, move the tolerance slider. Use the brush to erase leftover background or restore parts that were cut out by mistake. Finally pick a background and download the PNG.

Why use Image Background Remover

Plain backgrounds, such as a white wall, a studio sheet or a green screen, give the cleanest result. A transparent PNG works on product listings, thumbnails and presentations without any extra software.
