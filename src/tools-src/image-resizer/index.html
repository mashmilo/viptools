The Image Resizer changes the width and height of a photo or graphic in seconds. Enter exact pixel sizes or a percentage, and the tool redraws the image at the new size and lets you download it.

How to use the Image Resizer

Choose an image from your device. Enter a width or height and leave Keep aspect ratio ticked so the picture is not stretched. Or type a percentage such as 50 to make the image half its original size. Pick JPG for photos, PNG for graphics with transparency, or WebP for small files on websites. Press Download.

Why resize images

Oversized photos slow down websites, fill email attachments and get rejected by upload forms. Many platforms have fixed size rules, for example profile photos of 400 by 400 pixels or banners of 1500 by 500. Resizing to the exact size avoids the platform cropping or blurring your image.

Frequently asked questions

Does resizing make the image blurry? Shrinking keeps the image sharp. Enlarging a small image cannot add detail, so it will look soft.
