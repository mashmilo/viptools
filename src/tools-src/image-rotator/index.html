The Image Rotator turns a photo sideways or upside down and mirrors it left to right or top to bottom. It is handy for photos taken in the wrong orientation, scanned documents and mirrored selfies.

How to rotate or flip an image

Choose an image and use the buttons. Each rotate button turns the picture by 90 degrees. Flip horizontal mirrors it left to right, and flip vertical mirrors it top to bottom. The preview updates after each click, and Reset returns the image to its original state. When it looks right, press Download.

Why rotate a photo

Phone cameras sometimes save a picture sideways because of how the device was held, and some screenshots or scans come out rotated. Rotating fixes the orientation without re-editing the whole picture. Flipping is useful for mirrored selfies, which look wrong when posted, or for reversing a design or logo.

Choosing a download format

The tool exports JPG for photos and PNG if your image has transparency. Rotating a JPG and saving it again causes very slight quality loss each time, so rotate once and save the final result.

Frequently asked questions

Does rotating change the image size? No, the number of pixels stays the same. Rotating by 90 degrees swaps the width and height. Can I rotate a PNG with transparency? Yes. The tool keeps transparent areas in PNG files.
