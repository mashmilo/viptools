Turn any text into spoken audio with the voices your browser already has. Paste an article, a script or a note, pick a voice, and listen. Nothing is sent to a server.

How to use Text to Speech

Type or paste your text, choose a voice, then adjust the speed and pitch. Press Speak to listen and Stop to end playback early. Changing the voice or settings applies from the next time you press Speak.

Why use Text to Speech

Listening helps when you are reviewing your own writing, checking how a sentence sounds, studying while away from the screen, or making content more accessible to people who find long text tiring to read.
