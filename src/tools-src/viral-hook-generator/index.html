Strong hooks decide whether people stay for the rest of a video, post or article. Enter your topic, choose where you will publish, and get eight hook lines you can edit and reuse. Everything is generated in your browser.

    How to use Viral Hook Generator

    Describe your topic in a few words, pick the platform, and press Generate. Choose the line that fits your voice best, change the wording to match your audience, and use it as the first sentence of your video, caption or article.

    Why hooks matter

    On short-video apps and search results, people decide within seconds whether to keep watching or reading. A hook that names a problem, promises a clear result or raises a question gives viewers a reason to stay.
