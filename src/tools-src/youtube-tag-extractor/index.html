YouTube Tag Extractor

Paste a YouTube video link to see its hidden tags and hashtags, count the characters and copy them in one click.

Tags

Hashtags in the title and description

The YouTube Tag Extractor reads the tags that a creator added to a video and shows them as a list you can copy. It also lists the hashtags used in the title and description, and counts how many characters the tags use.

How to use the YouTube Tag Extractor

Paste the link of a public video and press Extract tags. The title, channel, upload date and view count appear first, followed by the tags. Use Copy tags to get them as a comma separated list, ready for the tags field in YouTube Studio.

Why use it

Tags are not shown on the YouTube page, so they are hard to see without a tool. Reading the tags of videos in your topic gives you ideas for keywords, common spellings and related phrases that you may have missed.
