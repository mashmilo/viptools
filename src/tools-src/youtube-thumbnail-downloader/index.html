YouTube Thumbnail Downloader

Paste a YouTube link to see and download its thumbnail in every available size, up to 1280x720 HD.

The YouTube Thumbnail Downloader shows every thumbnail size that exists for a video and lets you save the one you need as a JPG. It works with watch links, short links, Shorts, live and embed links, and with a plain video ID.

How to use the YouTube Thumbnail Downloader

Copy the link of the video from the address bar or the Share button in the YouTube app. Paste it in the box and press Get thumbnails. Each available size appears with its pixel dimensions. Press Download to save a file, or Open to view it full size in a new tab.

Why use it

Creators study thumbnails of top videos to learn what works, designers need a quick reference image, and bloggers need the right picture for a video review. Getting the file directly is faster than taking a screenshot, and the maximum resolution file is sharper than any screenshot of the player.
