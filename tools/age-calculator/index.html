Age Calculator

Calculate your exact age in years, months and days from your date of birth. Free, instant and private.

The Age Calculator shows your exact age from your date of birth, plus total days lived and days until your next birthday.

How to use Age Calculator

Pick your date of birth using the date field. Your age appears at once.

Why use Age Calculator

Exact age is needed for forms, exams, job applications, insurance and school admission. It is also fun to see how many days you have lived.

How it works

The calculator compares your date of birth with today's date and counts complete years, then the remaining months and days. Leap years are handled by the built-in date system.

Useful for

Checking eligibility for age-based rules, filling in forms that ask for exact age, and seeing how many days old a baby or pet is.

Frequently asked questions

Does it store my date of birth?

The tool runs in your browser. Your date is not sent to a server.

Is the result the same as my official age?

It is a calculation from the dates you enter. Official records and age rules may use different cut-off dates.

Can I calculate the age of someone else?

Yes. Enter any valid date of birth.
