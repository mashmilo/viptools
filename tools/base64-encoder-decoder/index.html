Base64 Encoder & Decoder

Encode text to Base64 or decode Base64 back to text.

Base64 turns data into plain text characters that are safe to send through systems that only handle text. This tool encodes and decodes it, with full Unicode support.

How to use Base64 Encoder & Decoder

Type or paste your input, then press Encode to produce Base64 or Decode to read it. Invalid input shows a clear message.

Why use Base64 Encoder & Decoder

Developers use Base64 for emails, data URLs, API tokens and configuration files. Doing it online is faster than writing code for a quick check.

Frequently asked questions

Is Base64 encryption? No, it is only an encoding and offers no security. Is my data uploaded? No. Is it free? Yes.
