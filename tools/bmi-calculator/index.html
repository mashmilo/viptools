BMI Calculator

Calculate your Body Mass Index (BMI) from weight in kilograms and height in centimeters. See the standard adult weight category instantly.

BMI compares your weight with your height to give a simple screening number. This calculator uses kilograms and centimeters.

How to use BMI Calculator

Enter your weight in kilograms and your height in centimeters. Your BMI and category appear immediately.

Why use BMI Calculator

BMI is a quick starting point for understanding weight status. It does not measure body fat or muscle, so it is not a diagnosis. Talk to a doctor for health advice.

How the formula works

BMI is your weight in kilograms divided by your height in meters squared. For example, 70 kg at 170 cm gives 70 ÷ (1.70 × 1.70) = 24.2, which is in the normal range for adults.

Limits of BMI

BMI does not separate fat from muscle, does not show where fat is stored, and can be misleading for athletes, older adults, pregnant people and some ethnic groups. Health professionals often use waist measurement and other checks alongside it.

When to talk to a doctor

If your BMI puts you in an unusual range, or you have weight concerns, see a doctor or registered dietitian. They can take your full health history into account.

Frequently asked questions

What BMI categories are used?

For adults, the commonly used ranges are: below 18.5 underweight, 18.5 to 24.9 normal, 25 to 29.9 overweight and 30 or above obesity.

Is BMI accurate for children?

No. Children and teenagers are assessed with BMI-for-age percentiles, which this calculator does not provide.

Is my data saved?

No. The calculation runs in your browser and nothing is sent to a server.
