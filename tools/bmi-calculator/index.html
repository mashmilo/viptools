BMI Calculator

Calculate your Body Mass Index from weight and height.

BMI compares your weight with your height to give a simple screening number. This calculator uses kilograms and centimeters.

How to use BMI Calculator

Enter your weight in kilograms and your height in centimeters. Your BMI and category appear immediately.

Why use BMI Calculator

BMI is a quick starting point for understanding weight status. It does not measure body fat or muscle, so it is not a diagnosis. Talk to a doctor for health advice.

Frequently asked questions

Is BMI accurate for athletes? Not always. Is it free? Yes.
