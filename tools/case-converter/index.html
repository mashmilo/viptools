Case Converter

Convert text to UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case or Sentence case instantly. Paste your text and copy the result. Free and private.

The Case Converter changes the capitalisation of any text in one click. It supports uppercase, lowercase, title case and sentence case, and runs privately in your browser.

How to use Case Converter

Paste your text into the box and press the button for the style you want. The text is replaced instantly, and you can switch styles as many times as you like.

Why use Case Converter

Fixing text typed with caps lock on, formatting headlines and tidying copied content by hand is slow. This tool saves time for writers, students, marketers and developers, and consistent capitalisation makes titles and headings look professional.

Example

"the quick brown fox" becomes "The Quick Brown Fox" in Title Case and "The quick brown fox" in Sentence case. Use uppercase for short labels and headings only, since long uppercase text is harder to read.

Tips for clean text

Convert text before you format it for a document or a website. Check headings in Title Case against your style guide, because rules for which small words stay lowercase differ between publishers.

Frequently asked questions

Does the converter change my original text?

It shows the converted text in the output box. Your original input is not sent anywhere.

What is Sentence case?

Sentence case capitalises only the first letter of each sentence, as in normal prose.

Is Title Case the same in every style guide?

No. Guides such as AP and Chicago differ on which short words stay lowercase, so this tool capitalises each word in a simple way.
