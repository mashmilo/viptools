Compound Interest Calculator

See how savings grow with compound interest over time. Enter principal, rate, years and compounding frequency for an estimate.

Compound interest earns interest on interest. This calculator shows your final balance for any amount, rate and period.

How to use Compound Interest Calculator

Enter the starting amount, annual rate, number of years and how often interest compounds.

Why use Compound Interest Calculator

Seeing the long-term effect helps with savings goals and with comparing deposits or investments. Starting early makes a big difference.

The formula

A = P (1 + r/n)^(nt), where P is the starting amount, r the annual rate, n the number of compounding periods per year and t the years. For example, 1,000 at 5% compounded monthly for 10 years grows to about 1,647.

Why time matters

Growth accelerates over time because interest is earned on earlier interest. Starting early often matters more than the exact rate. Remember that real investment returns vary and can be negative, unlike the steady rate in this estimate.

Frequently asked questions

Does this include taxes or fees?

No. The calculator shows a simple estimate. Taxes, fees and inflation can reduce real returns.

Is a higher compounding frequency always better?

It increases the result, but the effect is small compared with the rate and time.

Is this financial advice?

No. It is a general estimate. Speak to a qualified adviser before making investment decisions.
