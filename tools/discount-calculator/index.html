Discount Calculator

Calculate the sale price and the amount you save from a percentage discount. Quick, accurate and free.

Enter a price and a discount percentage to see the final price and your savings at once.

How to use Discount Calculator

Type the original price and the discount percent. Results update as you type.

Why use Discount Calculator

Sales can be confusing. Checking the real price helps you compare offers and shop smarter.

Example

A 200 item with a 25% discount costs 150 and saves you 50. The discount is 200 × 0.25 = 50, and the sale price is 200 − 50 = 150.

Stacked discounts

Two discounts applied one after another are not the same as adding their percentages. A 20% discount followed by 10% gives 200 × 0.8 × 0.9 = 144, which is 28% off, not 30%.

Frequently asked questions

Does this include sales tax?

No. Add tax after the discount if your local rules apply it to the sale price.

How do I work out the original price?

Divide the sale price by (1 minus the discount rate). For example, 150 at 25% off was 150 ÷ 0.75 = 200.

Is the tool free?

Yes, with no sign-up.
