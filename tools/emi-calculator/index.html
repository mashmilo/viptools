EMI Loan Calculator

Estimate your monthly loan EMI, total interest and total payment for home, car or personal loans. Adjust amount, rate and term.

See your monthly instalment, total interest and total repayment for any loan before you apply.

How to use EMI Loan Calculator

Enter the loan amount, the yearly interest rate and the number of years. Results update instantly.

Why use EMI Loan Calculator

Comparing loan offers is easier when you know the real monthly cost. It helps with home, car and personal loan planning.

The EMI formula

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)^n ÷ ((1 + r)^n − 1), where P is the loan amount, r is the monthly interest rate (annual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100) and n is the number of monthly payments. For example, a 500,000 loan at 9% for 5 years gives an EMI of about 10,379 per month.

Using the result

Compare total interest between offers, not just the monthly figure. A longer term lowers the EMI but usually raises the total interest paid. Check the bank's exact quote before you borrow, since fees and rounding rules can change the final figures.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a bank quote?

No. It is an estimate from the amount, rate and term you enter. Lenders may add fees, insurance or different rounding.

Does a longer loan term always cost more?

It usually does. Stretching the term lowers each payment but adds interest over more months.

Does the calculator store my numbers?

No. The calculation runs in your browser.
