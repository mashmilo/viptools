Image Compressor

Compress and resize JPG and PNG images in your browser. Adjust quality, see the size saving and download. Images never leave your device.

Shrink JPG and PNG photos to a smaller size right in your browser. Your images are never uploaded.

How to use Image Compressor

Choose an image, adjust the quality slider and download the compressed JPG.

Why use Image Compressor

Smaller images make websites load faster, improve SEO and save storage and data.

How it works

The image is drawn onto a canvas in your browser and re-encoded as JPG at the quality you choose. Lower quality gives a smaller file with more visible artefacts. Values from about 60 to 80 often give a good balance for photos.

Transparency note

JPG does not support transparency. Transparent areas of a PNG become white in the output. Keep the original PNG if you need a transparent background.

Frequently asked questions

Are my images uploaded?

No. Compression happens in your browser, and the file never leaves your device.

Why is the output always JPG?

JPG compresses photos much more than PNG. The tool outputs JPG, so PNG transparency is replaced with white.

Does compression help SEO?

Smaller images load faster, which helps page speed. Google treats page experience as one of many signals, so compressing images is good practice rather than a guaranteed ranking boost.
