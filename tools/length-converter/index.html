Convert any length between metric and imperial units instantly, including meters, kilometers, miles, yards, feet and inches.

How to use Length Converter

Enter a value, choose the unit to convert from and the unit to convert to. The result updates as you type.

Why use Length Converter

Travel, construction, school work and online shopping all mix metric and imperial units. A fast converter avoids costly mistakes.

Example

One mile equals 1.609344 kilometers exactly. To convert feet to meters, multiply by 0.3048. A 6-foot person is therefore 1.8288 meters tall.

Metric and imperial

Most of the world uses metric units, while the United States mainly uses imperial units for everyday measures such as height and road distance. Converters help when a product spec or travel guide uses the other system.
