Percentage Calculator

Calculate a percentage of a number, what percent one number is of another, or the percentage change between two values.

Solve everyday percentage problems quickly: find a percent of a number, or see what percent one value is of another.

How to use Percentage Calculator

Fill in the numbers in either row. Both answers update instantly.

Why use Percentage Calculator

Percentages appear in discounts, exam marks, taxes, tips and business reports. Calculating them online avoids errors.

Three common calculations

What is 15% of 200? That is 0.15 × 200 = 30. What percent is 30 of 200? That is 30 ÷ 200 × 100 = 15%. What is the change from 80 to 100? That is (100 − 80) ÷ 80 × 100 = 25% increase.

Why it matters

Percentages appear in discounts, tax, test scores, budgets and growth figures. Checking the base value, the number you are taking the percentage of, is the most common source of mistakes.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between percent of and percent change?

Percent of finds a share of a number. Percent change compares a new value with an old value.

Why is my percentage change not the same going up and down?

Percentages depend on the starting value. A 50% increase followed by a 50% decrease does not return you to the original number.

Does it round the results?

Results are shown with a few decimal places. Round further if your use case needs fewer.
