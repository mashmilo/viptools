Remove Duplicate Lines

Remove duplicate lines from any list, keeping the first occurrence. Clean email lists, keywords and logs in your browser.

Clean any list by removing repeated lines while keeping the original order.

How to use Remove Duplicate Lines

Paste your list with one item per line and press the button. The cleaned list appears below.

Why use Remove Duplicate Lines

Email lists, keyword lists, URLs and data exports often contain duplicates. Removing them improves accuracy and saves effort.

Example

Pasting the lines "apple", "pear", "apple", "fig" returns "apple", "pear", "fig". The first occurrence stays in its original position, so the order of your list is preserved.

Typical uses

Clean a keyword list before uploading it to an ad platform, merge contact lists, or tidy a copied log file before analysing it.

Frequently asked questions

Does it sort the lines?

No. It removes repeats and keeps the original order.

Is the comparison case-sensitive?

Check the output carefully if case matters, since "Apple" and "apple" may be treated as different lines depending on the setting.

Does it upload my list?

No. The cleaning happens in your browser.
