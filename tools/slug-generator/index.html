Slug Generator

Turn any title into a clean, lowercase URL slug with hyphens. Remove accents and special characters for SEO-friendly web addresses.

A slug is the readable end part of a URL. This tool converts any title into a short, lowercase, hyphenated slug.

How to use Slug Generator

Type your title. The slug appears instantly and is ready to copy.

Why use Slug Generator

Clean URLs are easier for people to read and for search engines to understand, which supports good SEO.

Example

"10 Best Tips for Better Sleep!" becomes "10-best-tips-for-better-sleep". Lowercase letters, numbers and hyphens are safe and readable in web addresses.

SEO tips for slugs

Keep slugs short and descriptive, and include the main keyword once. Avoid dates and IDs unless your content needs them. Changing a slug later requires a redirect from the old address.

Frequently asked questions

Why are slugs lowercase with hyphens?

Lowercase letters with hyphens are readable and consistent. Underscores and mixed case can cause duplicate-content issues on some servers.

Does the slug change accented letters?

The generator aims to convert accented letters to plain ASCII equivalents, so URLs stay simple.

Does it affect ranking directly?

Google has said that a descriptive URL can help people and search engines understand a page, but the effect is small. Clear content matters much more.
