Unix Timestamp Converter

Convert Unix epoch timestamps to human-readable dates and back. Shows the current timestamp in seconds. Free developer tool.

Unix time counts seconds since 1 January 1970. This tool converts those numbers into dates and dates into timestamps.

How to use Unix Timestamp Converter

Enter a timestamp to see its UTC date, or choose a date to get its timestamp. Press the button for the current time.

Why use Unix Timestamp Converter

Developers meet timestamps in logs, databases and APIs. A quick converter makes debugging far easier.

What a Unix timestamp is

A Unix timestamp counts seconds since 1 January 1970 at 00:00:00 UTC. For example, 0 is the epoch itself, and 1000000000 is 9 September 2001 at 01:46:40 UTC.

Common pitfalls

Some systems store milliseconds rather than seconds, so a value 1000 times larger than expected usually means milliseconds. Always check the time zone, since a timestamp is UTC while the displayed date may use local time.

Frequently asked questions

Is the timestamp in seconds or milliseconds?

This converter treats the input as seconds. Divide a millisecond timestamp by 1000 before converting.

Which time zone does the output use?

Convert and check the result against UTC and your local time, since the display can differ depending on your device settings.

Does the tool send my data anywhere?

No. Conversions run in your browser.
