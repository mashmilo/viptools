YouTube Playlist Length Calculator

Estimate total playlist watch time at different speeds.

Total watch time at each speed

The YouTube Playlist Length Calculator tells you exactly how long a playlist takes to watch. Paste a playlist link and the tool reads the real length of every video through the official YouTube Data API, then adds them up. You see the total at normal speed, at 1.25x, 1.5x, 1.75x and 2x, plus a custom speed of your own. It is built for students working through long courses, people planning a binge of lectures or podcasts, and anyone who wants to know whether a playlist fits into their week.

How to use the Playlist Length Calculator

Open a playlist on YouTube, copy the address from the browser bar or the Share button, and paste it into the box above. A link that looks like youtube.com/playlist?list=PL followed by letters and numbers works, and so does a video link that contains a list parameter. You can also paste the bare playlist ID. Press the button and wait a few seconds. Very long playlists with hundreds of videos need a little more time because the videos are loaded in batches of fifty.

Advanced options explained

Use the From and To boxes to measure only part of a playlist, for example videos 10 to 25 of a course. The skip fields remove very short or very long videos, which is handy for ignoring trailers, intros or three hour livestream recordings. Enter your own watch speed to see a custom total, and enter how many minutes you can watch per day to get a finish plan with the number of days and a finish date. You can sort the video list by playlist order, longest first or shortest first, copy a text summary, or download the whole list as a CSV file that opens in Excel or Google Sheets.

How the total is calculated

The calculator adds the duration of each available video in your chosen range and divides the result by the playback speed. At 2x speed a ten hour playlist takes five hours. Private, deleted and region blocked videos cannot be measured, so they are left out and reported in a note under the title. Live streams that have not finished and upcoming premieres have no fixed length and are skipped as well.

Tips for finishing long playlists

Set a daily time budget and let the plan tell you how many days you need. Most spoken content stays easy to follow at 1.25x or 1.5x, which saves hours across a large course. Use the longest first sort to find the videos that deserve a dedicated session, and use the range option to split a huge playlist into weekly chunks.

Frequently asked questions

Does it work with private playlists? No. Only public and unlisted playlists can be read, and private playlists, Watch Later and Liked videos are not available. Is there a video limit? The tool loads up to 3,000 videos per playlist. Is the result accurate? Durations come straight from YouTube and are shown to the second, though YouTube may round some videos by a fraction of a second. Is it free? Yes, it is free, needs no sign up and works on phones, tablets and computers.
